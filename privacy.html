Wordnotch — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Craftbit Studios · sohaibajmalp@gmail.com

Wordnotch is a word ladder puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It is short because the app does little with your data.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or any contact details.
  • Your progress, coins, streak and settings are stored on your device only. We never see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. The ads SDK collects the data described below, under Google's privacy policy, and you can limit it.
  • The app downloads puzzle packs from our content server.
  • The app sends anonymous gameplay statistics (which level was played, how long it took, whether a hint was used) through Google Analytics for Firebase, so we can see where players get stuck and improve the puzzles. No account, name or contact details are attached to them.

What stays on your device

Everything about how you play: solved levels, the puzzle you are in the middle of, coin balance, daily streak, statistics, theme and sound preferences. This is kept in the app's private storage. It is not backed up to our servers, because we have none. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Wordnotch is free and supported by ads: banners on the menu screens, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and rewarded videos you choose to watch for coins. Ads are served by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To do this, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) may be derived;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped or watched them.

This data is collected and used by Google and its advertising partners, not by us. How Google uses it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • Anywhere, you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings under Google → Ads.
  • Removing ads through the in-app purchase stops banners, full-screen and app-open ads. Rewarded videos remain available because you start them yourself.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To make the puzzles better we record a small number of gameplay events: which level was opened, solved or abandoned, how long it took, how many backtracks, whether a hint, an extra rung or an ad was used, and when the "stuck" prompt appeared. Each event carries the level's id and counts — never the words you placed, never your coin balance.

Firebase attaches to these events a random app-instance identifier it generates on your device, plus device information (model, operating system, language) and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This is described in Google's Privacy Policy. We use the data only in aggregate — "level 37 is abandoned by 40% of players" — never to look at an individual.

You can reset the app-instance identifier by clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app.

Puzzle content downloads

The app ships with its puzzles built in and plays fully offline. When a connection is available it may check our content server for new puzzle packs. That request carries no account or personal information; like any web server, ours records the requesting IP address and the file requested in short-lived access logs used only to keep the service running.

You can stop downloads on mobile data with Settings → Sync on mobile data.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and other purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or bank details. The app receives only a confirmation that a product was bought, which it uses to grant what you paid for on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you that a daily puzzle is ready or that a streak is about to lapse. These are generated on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to produce them, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's settings or in Android's notification settings.

Children

Wordnotch is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone, except that the advertising data described above is collected directly by Google under its own policies.

Security

The app stores its data in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Network requests use HTTPS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
